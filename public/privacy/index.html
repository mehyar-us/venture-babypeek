🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address — when you enter it to unlock your portrait or receive updates. Used to deliver your purchase and, occasionally, to tell you about BabyPeek news.
  • AI-generated baby images — the portraits our AI creates for you, so we can show them back to you and let you download them.
  • Basic technical data — IP address (for rate limiting) and payment metadata from Stripe (we never see or store your card number).

What we never store

Your parent photos are processed in memory and never saved. The moment your portrait is generated, your uploads are gone. We cannot show them back to you, share them, or leak them — they simply don't exist on our servers.

Emails & unsubscribe

If we email you, every message includes a one-click unsubscribe link. Clicking it immediately opts you out — no login, no questions. You can also write to info@mehyar.us and ask to be removed or to delete your data.

Sharing

We don't sell your data. Email addresses live in our shared contact store (also used by our sister products) so you only ever have to unsubscribe once. Payment processing is handled by Stripe under their own privacy policy.

Children

BabyPeek is for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy? info@mehyar.us
